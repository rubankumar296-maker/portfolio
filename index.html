01 About me

About me

I like the part of analytics where the answer is not obvious yet: the messy data, the questions that need refining, and the moment a dashboard makes something click.

I'm Ruban Kumar N, an entry-level data analyst based in Chennai. My work sits at the intersection of Python, SQL (MySQL), and Power BI — from validating raw data to building reports that support real decisions.

I've built three end-to-end projects across retail, sports, and finance — 51,290 sales transactions, 1,000 customer financial profiles, and 54,600 player-match records. Each one starts with a raw dataset, moves through cleaning, exploration, and SQL analysis, and ends with an interactive Power BI report and documented findings.

I'm early in my career, but I work like an analyst: validate the data before trusting it, define metrics carefully, and explain what the numbers can — and cannot — tell you.

05 Process

Process

How I work Ask → Prepare → Analyse → Explain
01

Ask

Frame the business question before touching the data. A good analysis starts with the decision, not the dataset.

02

Prepare

Clean, validate, and document. I check the grain, the gaps, and the assumptions before trusting a total.

03

Analyse

Use Python and SQL to explore patterns, compare segments, and find the metrics that actually move the story.

04

Explain

Turn the findings into an interactive report with clear takeaways, sensible caveats, and next steps.
